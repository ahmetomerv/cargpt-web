1. Overview

CarGPT is designed with your privacy in mind. This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled. In short: we do not collect, store, or share any personal information that can identify you. The app processes car photos for identification purposes only, with no server storage or user tracking that links to your identity. It is built to function transparently and securely while protecting your privacy.

2. Minimal Data Collection (Anonymous Analytics)

To improve app performance and understand how features are used, CarGPT uses Firebase Analytics. This tool collects anonymous usage data only.

  • We track screen views and feature usage events (e.g. which page is used, how long a feature is interacted with).
  • No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected — this includes no names, IP addresses, email addresses, device IDs, or car photos.
  • Data collected cannot be linked back to individual users.
  • Analytics are solely used to improve the app experience and feature performance.

3. No User Accounts or Personal Information

  • Does not require or support user registration
  • Does not collect personal information such as name, email, or contact details
  • Does not share data with third parties

4. Photo Processing and Car Identification

CarGPT processes car photos solely to provide car identification services.

  • Photo access is strictly limited to car identification purposes
  • No photos are uploaded, stored, or shared on our servers
  • Photos are processed temporarily for AI analysis and then discarded
  • Access is granted only after explicit user consent
  • All car identification results are processed locally on your device

5. Local Device Storage

  • Car identification results and history
  • Saved car collections (if enabled by user)
  • No sensitive or personal data is stored externally

7. No Third-Party SDKs or Advertisements

Aside from Firebase Analytics (anonymous usage tracking as detailed above), CarGPT does not integrate any third-party SDKs or services. Specifically:

  • No advertising networks
  • No crash reporting or error tracking tools
  • No social media integrations
  • No data sharing with third-party AI services

8. Children's Privacy

CarGPT does not collect personal data and is safe for users of all ages. However, the app is intended for car enthusiasts, buyers, and collectors interested in vehicle identification.

9. Changes to This Policy

If there are changes to data handling practices, we will update this Privacy Policy. Updates will be posted within the app and/or on the App Store listing. Continued use of CarGPT implies acceptance of the updated terms.
